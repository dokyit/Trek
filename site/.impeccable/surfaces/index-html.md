# Home page (index.html)

Scope: the one landing page of trekdesktop.com. Mode: Persuade.

Who must act: a developer who already runs coding agents and has more than one piece of work going. What they should do: download Trek. What earns it: a first screen that says what Trek is in one line, a short story of how the Inbox behaves, and the real app at its real size. No testimonials, logos or numbers exist, and the page is built not to need them.

Must stay: few words, in short plain sentences, claiming only what the README says; real captures only, shown 1:1; staged content said to be staged; no trackers; reads whole without scripts and with Reduce Motion on. The hiker appears only in the story, never in the first screen, and the mark is never scenery.

Open: the iPhone app is built from source only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One calm photograph and two short sentences, then one small stage where the Inbox acts as you scroll. It refuses the category's arrangement of a dark page, a shrunken window under the headline and a row of agent logos, and it refuses a wall of copy.

OWN-WORLD: A real photograph of a trail up a golden hill under a pale dawn sky, and a dusk grade of the same frame for Night. Below it Paper by day and near-black at night, as the app's two themes. Ember marks the action and a thread at work, amber a thread that waits and emerald one that has finished. Nothing else is coloured. Bricolage Grotesque at 600 for display, Hanken Grotesk at 400 and 500 for text, no italics. Hairlines, soft radii, two soft shadows (under the Inbox rows and under the phone). The mark is a faceted mountain with a dotted trail and a summit flag. The 12 by 16 pixel hiker and his dotted trail.

STORY: The visitor reads what Trek is and can download at once. Scrolling, they watch three threads start, one stop to ask, the phone offer the same question, the thread go back to work and finished work wait. Then they see the app itself, that agents can run an iOS app, and where each build is. The iPhone in the story is a reduced picture of a real capture, the one place a capture is not shown 1:1, because it is a prop beside the stage and not a product shot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The photograph fills the screen with its horizon at 70% of the height. In its sky, centred: a two-line headline near 72 px, two short sentences, one filled Download button and one fine line with the version. The bar holds the mark, the name, two links and Download. Nothing else.

FORM: Pinned by the owner in his own words (one calm scene with one headline and one button; a pinned stage where one small product object acts as you scroll); the roll was run afterwards for the record and its dealt structures and challengers were set aside, because his pin decides. seed:7715a7e226de6125. Signature interaction: the pinned stage, played forwards and backwards by scrolling, one line of caption at a time. Motion grammar: captions and rows arrive by opacity, a short rise and a blur that clears; rows glide over 420 ms, ease-out; the hiker walks on a 16-frames-a-second clock; the first screen's copy rises in once, the question card comes and goes, Allow is pressed once, the phone slides in for its one line, and a working row's dot turns. Nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
