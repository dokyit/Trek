Ask an agent: "Make Trek feel like a campfire: warm ember colours, answers in a serif, soft round corners, a little tent as my prompt, and give the hiker an orange pack."

# Campfire

A whole look, in the dark and in the light. Night is ember on woodsmoke; day is parchment with a
burnt-orange accent. Answers are set in Spectral, everything else in Inter; corners are round
and the window is set roomy; what you write reads as a line after a small tent, which is also
the composer's prompt; the hiker's pack is ember orange. While you type, TODO glows and FIXME is
underlined in red.

What it shows: a `[[theme]]` (the `trek-theme` format: colours for both modes, fonts, corners,
lines, motion, the message style, the prompt mark and the hiker) and `[[effect]]`s over the
composer's draft.

Make it yours: every colour is in `campfire.toml`; change `user_mark` and `prompt` to any mark of
up to three characters.
